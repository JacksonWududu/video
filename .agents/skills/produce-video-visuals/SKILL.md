## Preconditions

- The validated narration master and exact audio-timed storyboard are locked.
- `per-shot-visual-direction-review-v3` is approved and checksum-current for every new or modified shot: each row locks machine scene class, white cat, structure/treatment, final active route, and route-resolved visible-text mode/exact copy/placement. Approved rows accept only `none` or `required`; ordinary `imagegen` with `white_cat_present: true` and every `xuan-paper-diorama` shot are forced to `none` with null copy/placement and no top title. Completed unchanged v1/v2 evidence is legacy read-only and cannot authorize generation, revision, or reapproval. Any stale or pending mapping or character reference blocks its dependents.
- Storyboard Review is `approved`. Reread the active storyboard file and recompute its exact-byte SHA-256; require it to match the recorded active, presented, and approved SHA-256 values. When they match, atomically set phase `visual_production` before generating the first asset. A missing, `pending`, `changes_requested`, stale, or checksum-mismatched Review is a blocker; archive and clear stale active approval evidence, set phase `storyboard_construction`, and return to `$build-video-storyboard` without generating or modifying any visual asset, including images, master frames, action variants, data/transition graphics, covers, prompts rendered into assets, or derivatives.
- Every shot has a class, real duration, semantic beat, framing, safe area, and required props/data.
- AI-generated storyboard sources target `16:9` landscape and must measure within `0.5%` relative aspect-ratio error; the Remotion composition remains exact `1920×1080`. Only after exact-byte approval may a source become a separate composition derivative through proportional scale-to-cover and the smallest centered edge crop within that tolerance; never stretch or pad.
- The canonical shared-library white-cat reference and character bible are available and valid when the cat appears.

## Produce narrative masters

1. Resolve the approved `treatment_profile_id` as visual-language authority and consume its structure-derived `composition_layout_id` plus passing treatment-layout compatibility as prompt-layout evidence; never turn that evidence into an XHS card. Keep `loose-line-vivid-watercolor` only for the corresponding ordinary imagegen profile. For `xuan-paper-diorama` or `ink-doodle-knowledge-card`, reread the complete `$generate-visual-styles` Skill and the route's checksum-pinned profile, require their current SHA-256 values to equal the route catalog, and follow the matching route contract under `leverage-video/src/shared/visual-generation-routes/`; a mismatch blocks generation and requires a refreshed route review. Supply the canonical white-cat v2 image as an actual image input to every white-cat master generation and every action-variant edit; metadata alone does not satisfy identity lock.
2. Convert each Chinese visual specification into a concise structured English production prompt. Preserve culturally specific Chinese concepts bilingually when English alone could change meaning.
3. State the exact prompt constraint `16:9 landscape composition`, scene, subject, action, emotion, camera/framing, geometry, props, style, identity invariants, composition invariants, subtitle-safe area, and avoid rules. Also pass an explicit 16:9 aspect-ratio or size parameter when the generator supports one; prompt text alone is not verification.
4. Generate only the currently unlocked versioned master non-destructively, then inspect it at full resolution and thumbnail scale. Follow `visual-asset-review-v2`: strict items pause alone; normal items pause in ordered batches of four, before a strict barrier, or at queue end.
5. Make one targeted correction at a time and retry from the valid source rather than accepting identity or composition drift.

## Produce and lock the asset set

- Generate every required `action-state-schedule-v2` family from the real shot frames. Approve the immutable master as strict first; then generate each independent master-derived variant sequentially. Variants may join normal four-item review batches unless marked strict or revised.
- Dispatch every new or modified shot only by its approved active `visual_generation_route`: `imagegen` creates the default narrative illustrations, including comic-styled single images or separately generated sequential states; `xuan-paper-diorama` calls `$generate-visual-styles` with the exact checksum-pinned 宣纸微缩叠景 profile and creates text-free narrative PNGs; `ian-handdrawn-ppt` creates the default no-cat structured PNGs; `ink-doodle-knowledge-card` calls `$generate-visual-styles` with the exact checksum-pinned 墨线知识卡 profile and creates no-cat structured PNGs; `srt-whiteboard-animation` creates its approved panorama/annotation/preview/clip set. `comic-imagegen` and `doodle-slides` are retired and must block new or modified production. A white cat may use `imagegen` or `xuan-paper-diorama`, always with the canonical reference. Ordinary white-cat `imagegen` and all 宣纸 route outputs are text-free; no-cat ordinary imagegen, Ian, Ink Doodle, and Whiteboard each follow their own route text contract. Any route mismatch blocks production; do not infer, default, substitute, or normalize a route.
- For every `xuan-paper-diorama` or `ink-doodle-knowledge-card` state, record `generator: codex-native-imagegen`, the locked prompt path/checksum, every actual reference checksum, `style_profile_id`, style-profile checksum, and Skill checksum. Require a separately exact-byte-approved 1920×1080 PNG; normalization or revision creates a new approval object. Never simulate either route with an overlay, shader, SVG/HTML redraw, or Remotion reconstruction.
- For an approved structured Ian shot, `ian-handdrawn-ppt` creates the complete raster through `ian-knowledge-video-frame-v1`: bind the current v3 review path/checksum/map hash, create exactly one 1920×1080 PNG per queue item with the ordinary Ian page shell disabled, then run its validator. `none` forbids all visible text; `required` permits only the exact approved Chinese and placement. The accepted Ian or Ink Doodle PNG is the final production visual. Do not create an editable data/logic scene or let Remotion regenerate its content.
- Keep completed `comic-imagegen` and `doodle-slides` assets as historical read-only evidence only. Never generate, revise, normalize, reapprove, or derive a new/revoice asset from either route; a requested change must return to Visual Direction Review and choose an active route.
- For `srt-whiteboard-animation`, require three nested approvals in one queue item in this order: `source_image_review`, `annotation_review`, then `clip_review`. Keep later queue items locked until all three pass. A change at any stage invalidates only that stage and its downstream Whiteboard derivatives. New/modified work uses `whiteboard-annotation-v2` with the same `none | required` enum as v3 and a disk-verified v3 path/checksum/map binding. Narrative Whiteboard rejects all visible text; structured Whiteboard permits only exact storyboard-approved Chinese and placement, deterministically overlaid before source-image approval. Preserve the bottom subtitle safe area.
- For `ink-doodle-knowledge-card`, follow `ink-doodle-knowledge-card-route-v1`: bind the current v3 review, exact text contract, prompt checksum, actual reference checksums, and pinned Skill/profile fingerprints. Generate each state separately through `$generate-visual-styles`; approve only the final 1920×1080 PNG bytes. Remotion must not redraw its lettering, arrows, labels, or illustrations.
- Do not create an editable data/logic scene, SVG/HTML/canvas/code-native redraw, layer-by-layer reconstruction, or separate editable animation for a matching visual. After approval, create only the exact 1920×1080 normalized raster required by the canvas contract. Remotion may consume that raster for timeline placement and non-redrawing animation effects, but it may not generate or replace the image.
- Revalidate and archive the exact shared `cover.png` bytes according to `cover-only-v1`, then create only its deterministic 1920×1080 normalized raster. Do not generate, redraw, relabel, or add a text overlay to the opening cover, and do not place it in the per-image review queue.
- Require every accepted AI-generated storyboard source to be landscape and within `0.5%` relative error of `16:9`. Reject anything else before presentation. After source approval, create a separate exact `1920×1080` derivative through deterministic proportional scale-to-cover and minimal centered crop within tolerance; never stretch or pad.
- Decode every active raster, including every Ian handdrawn data/logic visual and the shared opening cover. Measure every master, action variant, data/logic raster, other transition asset, cover, and normalized raster rather than trusting prompt metadata or inherited state.
- Write the accepted manifest with paths, hashes, dimensions, prompt/reference checksums, v3 direction evidence, `action-state-schedule-v2`, review mode, per-asset approvals, and batch-manifest hashes. 宣纸与墨线知识卡 items additionally bind the exact style profile/Skill fingerprints; Ian items must include the passing `ian-knowledge-video-frame-v1` manifest/evidence. Require exact shot/asset/review route equality, reject retired `comic-imagegen` and `doodle-slides`, and preserve Whiteboard evidence.
- Preserve every non-Whiteboard shot's ordered `image_sequence`. For `N` accepted images or states, write exactly `N - 1` consecutive `intra_shot_transitions`, each locked to `intra-shot-watercolor-bloom-v1`. A Whiteboard shot instead records one panorama and `whiteboard-element-sequence-replaces-action-family-v1`; its `intra_shot_transitions` is empty because drawing progression is already encoded in the approved MP4.
- Preserve every storyboard `scene-transition-v3` decision exactly. Registered `cut` retains zero duration/options and is never `none`; visible effects retain approved 0.3–0.6-second timing/options. Preserve class, source/next routes, route-first or shared-fallback recommendation authority/rule ID, recommendation, renderer, exact message/time, and map checksum. Opening hard cut and terminal clean hold remain fixed exemptions.
- Mark replaced images rejected or superseded. Downstream assembly may consume only the active accepted set.
- Hand the locked manifest and asset set to `$assemble-video-master` only after every queued visual asset has exact-byte user approval, the shared validator has reread current disk bytes and emitted a passing `visual-assets-lock-verification-v1`, and phase is `visual_assets_locked`.
