## Preconditions

- The validated narration master and exact audio-timed storyboard are locked.
- The exact readable v1 or current `white-cat-visual-style-selection-v2` is locked. Its episode-local profile checksum, mapped treatment, cohesion ID, and selection SHA-256 equal the top-level v3 direction binding and every row/selection; mixed, missing, or substituted bindings block. The publishing-cover package itself is not a queue asset.
- `per-shot-visual-direction-review-v3` is approved and checksum-current: `approved` in manual mode or policy-bound `policy_authorized` in one-click mode for every new or modified shot. Each row locks machine scene class, white cat, structure/treatment, final active route, route-resolved visible-text mode/exact copy/placement, and the exact absolute source path when the route is `local-video-file`. Authorized rows accept only `none` or `required`; ordinary `imagegen` with `white_cat_present: true`, every `xuan-paper-diorama` shot, and every local-video shot are forced to `none` with null copy/placement and no top title. Completed unchanged v1/v2 evidence is legacy read-only and cannot authorize generation, revision, or reapproval. Any stale or pending mapping, policy binding, or character reference blocks its dependents.
- New work requires checksum-current `storyboard-visual-rhythm-v2` and `action-state-schedule-v4`, including exact density selection/hash, fallback, split assessment, counts/order, UTF-8 mapping, clean frames, and transition map. Legacy v1/v3 is read-only.
- Storyboard Review is `approved` in manual mode or checksum-current and policy-bound `policy_authorized` in one-click mode. Reread the active storyboard file and recompute its exact-byte SHA-256; require it to match the recorded active, presented, and approved/policy-authorized SHA-256 values. When they match, atomically set phase `visual_production` before generating the first asset. A missing, `pending`, `changes_requested`, stale, checksum-mismatched, or policy-mismatched Review is a blocker; archive and clear stale active authorization evidence, set phase `storyboard_construction`, and return to `$build-video-storyboard` without generating or modifying any visual asset, including images, master frames, action variants, data/transition graphics, covers, prompts rendered into assets, or derivatives.
- Every shot has a class, real duration, semantic beat, framing, safe area, and required props/data.
- AI-generated storyboard sources target `16:9` landscape and must measure within `0.5%` relative aspect-ratio error; the Remotion composition remains exact `1920×1080`. Only after exact-byte approval may a source become a separate composition derivative through proportional scale-to-cover and the smallest centered edge crop within that tolerance; never stretch or pad.
- The canonical shared-library white-cat reference and character bible are available and valid when the cat appears.

## Produce narrative masters

Before opening any generator, order active queue items as all non-`local-video-file` assets first, preserving their normal dependency order, followed by all local-video items in storyboard order. Generate, QA, present, and approve the generated/Whiteboard assets first. Do not read, probe, copy, or retime a selected local video while any non-local item remains unapproved. A local-video item remains `pending_generation` as a deferred import target; it is not skipped from the final approval/lock requirement.

For every white-cat ImageGen item, use the exact episode-selected core, cover-derived, or registered-custom profile and matching treatment. Add exact prompt markers `WHITE-CAT VISUAL STYLE: <style_id>.` and `EPISODE VISUAL COHESION: <visual_cohesion_profile_id>.`; v2 additionally requires `EPISODE STYLE PROFILE SHA256: <style_profile_checksum_sha256>.` Apply the bound cohesion profile to all new prompts. Cover-derived or registered style transfers the full described style envelope to ordinary ImageGen only. Ian inherits only the selected palette, luminance, and negative-space envelope; never import cover typography, subjects, layout, the cat medium, animation modeling, glow, dense ornament, dark background, or a second Style Anchor.

1. Resolve the approved `treatment_profile_id` as visual-language authority and consume its structure-derived `composition_layout_id` plus passing treatment-layout compatibility as prompt-layout evidence; never turn that evidence into an XHS card. Keep `loose-line-vivid-watercolor` only for the corresponding ordinary imagegen profile. For `xuan-paper-diorama` or `ink-doodle-knowledge-card`, reread the complete `$generate-visual-styles` Skill and the route's checksum-pinned profile, require their current SHA-256 values to equal the route catalog, and follow the matching route contract under `leverage-video/src/shared/visual-generation-routes/`; a mismatch blocks generation and requires a refreshed route review. Supply the canonical white-cat v2 image as an actual image input to every white-cat master generation and every action-variant edit; metadata alone does not satisfy identity lock.
2. Convert each Chinese visual specification into a concise structured English production prompt. Preserve culturally specific Chinese concepts bilingually when English alone could change meaning.
3. State the exact prompt constraint `16:9 landscape composition`, scene, subject, action, emotion, camera/framing, geometry, props, style, identity invariants, composition invariants, subtitle-safe area, and avoid rules. Apply the bound `episode-visual-cohesion-v2` profile while preserving each route's approved medium and unique anchors. It never changes approved route/treatment/character/text/composition or existing generated, approved, cover, local-video, or revoice pixels. Also pass an explicit 16:9 aspect-ratio or size parameter when the generator supports one; prompt text alone is not verification.
4. Generate only the currently unlocked versioned asset and inspect it at full resolution and thumbnail scale. Every new or explicitly rebuilt white-cat ImageGen asset must carry `white-cat-anatomy-qa-v2` evidence conforming to `leverage-video/src/shared/visual-assets/schemas/white-cat-anatomy-qa-v2.schema.json`; historical exact-byte-approved white-cat Xuan assets remain read-only and cannot be rebuilt. Bind that evidence to the exact reviewed PNG; require a separate-byte, fully decodable PNG numbered limb map bound to the source SHA-256 and exact `F1/F2/H1/H2` IDs. Approval and asset-lock gates reread that map from disk; deletion, replacement, symlink substitution, or stale SHA-256 blocks progress. Manual follows `visual-asset-review-v2` unchanged. One-click follows `visual-asset-review-v3`/`one_click_final_review_v1`: all QA still runs; passing items become `qa_passed_pending_final_review` and only true dependencies unlock. Its final review payload binds each white-cat numbered-map path, SHA-256, source SHA-256, and limb IDs. After all pass, generate one current-digest image-rich unified review HTML containing every current asset and stop for complete exact-list review. A QA pass never grants user approval or `approved`; only the applicable manual or checksum-current final-page decision may do so.
5. Apply `storyboard-image-generation-attempt-limit-v1` to every generated storyboard-image route. Record each distinct non-repairable QA-rejected output with `leverage-video/src/shared/visual-assets/record-image-generation-qa-failure.py`. The same recorder classifies an Ian master as `ian-generation-layout-repair-disposition-v1` without incrementing the rejected count only when its semantic groups are intact and mutually separated and its sole failure code is `IAN_ZONE_GEOMETRY` or `IAN_ZONE_GEOMETRY_AND_SUBTITLE_SAFE_BAND`; pause another generation and route those exact bytes to `ian-pre-split-layout-repair-v1`. Semantic, text, identity, style, lineage, overlap, clipping, missing-group, or otherwise non-deterministically-repairable defects remain counted rejections. On the third counted rejected generation for one stable logical asset, stop every automatic retry, pause the queue, and require user takeover. Changing the prompt, reference, base, composition, model, route, filename, asset version, or revision never resets the count. Failed-before-generation calls and repairable Ian geometry findings do not count; one-click authorization cannot bypass the stop. Final package and Visual Asset Review QA remain unchanged and strict.
6. Make one targeted correction at a time only from a source that already passes white-cat P0 anatomy, P1 identity, and P2 accessory geometry. A P0/P1/P2-failed output is evidence only, never the next edit target. Return to the last fully valid source; after one failed topology repair from that base, change to a readable three-quarter composition and regenerate from canonical references rather than extending the same prompt. Also record every failed white-cat ImageGen QA with `leverage-video/src/shared/visual-assets/record-white-cat-imagegen-qa-failure.py`; an anatomy or satchel `reason` must begin with the most specific stable P0/P2 code.

## Produce and lock the asset set

- Generate each required v4 family from real-audio semantics and its exact rich/standard count. The hero background is a separate dependency and never a scheduled pose. One-click may generate dependent poses after the background passes QA, but neither background nor poses are approved until the final exact-list decision.
- Dispatch every new or modified shot only by its approved active `visual_generation_route`: `imagegen` creates the default narrative illustrations, including comic-styled single images or separately generated sequential states; `xuan-paper-diorama` calls `$generate-visual-styles` with the exact checksum-pinned 宣纸微缩叠景 profile and creates text-free no-cat narrative PNGs; `ian-handdrawn-ppt` creates the default no-cat structured PNGs as layered-scene packages; `ink-doodle-knowledge-card` calls `$generate-visual-styles` with the exact checksum-pinned 墨线知识卡 profile and creates no-cat structured PNGs; `srt-whiteboard-animation` creates its approved panorama/annotation/preview/clip set; `local-video-file` creates nothing and remains deferred until every non-local asset is approved. `comic-imagegen` and `doodle-slides` are retired and must block new or modified production. A white cat uses only `imagegen`, the canonical reference, and the episode-selected style snapshot. White-cat `imagegen`, all 宣纸 outputs, and local video are text-free; no-cat ordinary imagegen, Ian, Ink Doodle, and Whiteboard each follow their own route text contract. Any route mismatch, style mismatch, profile-checksum mismatch, or cohesion-binding mismatch blocks production; do not infer, default, substitute, or normalize it.
- After all generated visual items are approved and no batch/strict boundary is open, process deferred `local-video-file` items one at a time in storyboard order with `leverage-video/src/shared/local-video-match/import-local-video.mjs`. Resolve only the exact approved absolute `.mp4` path at that point; reject a symlink, non-file, empty source, path mismatch, anything other than one unrotated native 1920×1080 H.264 video stream, or failed full decode. Archive the exact source bytes under the episode `assets/video/user-source/`, mute/ignore source audio, and compute `playback_rate = source_duration_seconds / (target_duration_frames / 30)` under `local-video-match-v1`. Do not trim, loop, pad, interpolate, crop, stretch, redraw, or add text. Present the exact matched preview and source checksum as a strict per-item Visual Asset Review; only explicit approval may set it to `approved`.
- For every `xuan-paper-diorama` or `ink-doodle-knowledge-card` state, record `generator: codex-native-imagegen`, the locked prompt path/checksum, every actual reference checksum, `style_profile_id`, style-profile checksum, and Skill checksum. Require a separately exact-byte-approved 1920×1080 PNG; normalization or revision creates a new approval object. A new or rebuilt normalized 宣纸 PNG containing the white cat must run v2 P0 QA on those final bytes and may not inherit anatomy evidence from its pre-normalized source. Never simulate either route with an overlay, shader, SVG/HTML redraw, or Remotion reconstruction.
- For an approved structured Ian shot, `ian-handdrawn-ppt` creates `ian-knowledge-video-layered-scene-v2`: bind the current storyboard, v3 review path/checksum/map hash, exact narration source bytes, shot frames, approved visual-rhythm event map, and approved `ian-layered-scene-plan-v1`. Use `gpt-image-2` once to generate one complete text-free Ian master, then deterministically normalize and split it into one opaque background plus ordered full-canvas transparent pre-text layers, overlay exact approved text on its owning layer, and recomposite into one deterministic final-composite review PNG. If a complete text-free master has intact separated groups but misses only locked zone bounds, require an exact user instruction before `ian-pre-split-layout-repair-v1`; preserve its rejected-source evidence and allow only deterministic downscaling plus translation before the normal split. Run the layered-scene validator and recorder. V1 independent-member and old flattened-frame recorders are completed-history read-only. Entry motion and SFX are separate assembly evidence under `ian-layered-entry-effects-v2`; they never change package pixels. When contour/path reveal is selected, produce and approve a checksum-bound full-canvas SVG path asset rather than tracing raster edges at runtime. For every selected cue, preserve the shared source bytes and create a deterministic pre-trimmed stereo 44.1 kHz WAV derivative with exact source/trim/output checksums.
- Keep completed `comic-imagegen` and `doodle-slides` assets as historical read-only evidence only. Never generate, revise, normalize, reapprove, or derive a new/revoice asset from either route; a requested change must return to Visual Direction Review and choose an active route.
- For `srt-whiteboard-animation`, require three nested approvals in one queue item in this order: `source_image_review`, `annotation_review`, then `clip_review`. Keep later queue items locked until all three pass. A change at any stage invalidates only that stage and its downstream Whiteboard derivatives. New/modified work uses `whiteboard-annotation-v2` with the same `none | required` enum as v3 and a disk-verified v3 path/checksum/map binding. Narrative Whiteboard rejects all visible text; structured Whiteboard permits only exact storyboard-approved Chinese and placement, deterministically overlaid before source-image approval. Preserve the bottom subtitle safe area.
- For `ink-doodle-knowledge-card`, follow `ink-doodle-knowledge-card-route-v1`: bind the current v3 review, exact text contract, prompt checksum, actual reference checksums, and pinned Skill/profile fingerprints. Generate each state separately through `$generate-visual-styles`; approve only the final 1920×1080 PNG bytes. Remotion must not redraw its lettering, arrows, labels, or illustrations.
- Outside the Ian layered-scene contract, do not create an editable data/logic scene, SVG/HTML/canvas/code-native redraw, layer-by-layer reconstruction, or separate editable animation for a matching visual. Ian v2 layers are checksum-bound deterministic derivatives of the one approved complete master. Remotion may consume only approved route-owned bytes; the sole SVG exception is a checksum-bound reveal path under `ian-layered-entry-effects-v2`, which controls visibility but never redraws or replaces the layer.
- Never copy, normalize, generate, redraw, or queue a publishing cover in this phase. Its accepted 16:9/9:16 bytes stay under the episode's publishing-cover package and may serve only as checksum-bound style references when v2 selected them; they never become storyboard visuals, opening frames, Remotion inputs, masters, or internal QA roles.
- Require every accepted AI-generated storyboard source to be landscape and within `0.5%` relative error of `16:9`. Reject anything else before presentation. After source approval, create a separate exact `1920×1080` derivative through deterministic proportional scale-to-cover and minimal centered crop within tolerance; never stretch or pad.
- Decode every active raster, including each Ian source/normalized master, background, pre-text/final transparent semantic layer, deterministic final composite, and the shared opening cover. Measure dimensions and alpha roles from disk rather than trusting prompt metadata or inherited state; replay Ian normalization/split/text-overlay steps, recomposite each package, and require exact derivative and final-composite byte equality.
- Write the accepted manifest with the style/cohesion/profile-checksum binding, passing `episode-visual-cohesion-qa-v1`, v2 rhythm/v4 schedule density bindings, and the active review contract. Manual records per-item/batch approvals; one-click records the complete final exact-list digest, its current `final-production-asset-review-package-v1` binding, and the user decision. Any image/package member change invalidates that binding and requires a new digest-named image-rich HTML containing the full current set before reapproval. An Ian approval binds the package manifest, scene-plan checksum, every ordered member path/checksum/dimension/alpha role, and the final composite—not only the preview PNG. Publishing covers are excluded. Local-video, 宣纸、Ink、Ian、Whiteboard and all route-specific evidence remain mandatory.
- Preserve every generated v3 shot's ordered `image_sequence`. For `N` accepted images or states, copy exactly `N - 1` approved `intra-shot-transition-v1` entries without inference: default `cut` is zero frames; `watercolor-bloom` appears only with explicit map-bound approval and stays 18 frames. Validate at least 15 clean target frames after each effect. A Whiteboard shot instead records one panorama and `whiteboard-element-sequence-replaces-action-family-v1`; a local-video shot records `local-video-source-replaces-image-action-family-v1`. Both keep an empty raster transition map because progression is encoded in their approved MP4. Historical v2 families retain their old mandatory watercolor chain unchanged.
- Preserve every storyboard `scene-transition-v3` decision exactly. Registered `cut` retains zero duration/options and is never `none`; visible effects retain approved 0.3–0.6-second timing/options. Preserve class, source/next routes, route-first or shared-fallback recommendation authority/rule ID, recommendation, renderer, exact message/time, and map checksum. Opening hard cut and terminal clean hold remain fixed exemptions.
- Mark replaced images rejected or superseded. Downstream assembly may consume only the active accepted set.
- Hand the locked manifest and asset set to `$assemble-video-master` only after every queued visual asset has exact-byte user approval, the shared validator has reread current disk bytes and emitted a passing `visual-assets-lock-verification-v1`, and phase is `visual_assets_locked`.
